* Man page ASCII output

Simple way to output man page in ASCII only format (no any special symbols):

#+BEGIN_SRC bash
groff -P\-c -mandoc -Tascii file.1|col -bx > file.txt
#+END_SRC

~file.txt~ is formatted as you see man output on your screen.
